## Exploration prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: premium original logo exploration board for the software maker brand "xDev".
Primary request: Create THREE genuinely different, thoughtful logo concepts based on a compact custom lowercase x+d monogram, NOT a standalone X, not a four-petal symbol. The previous four-piece symmetrical X was rejected as too generic. Each concept must combine x and d into ONE legible, memorable bespoke symbol with distinctive negative space, paired with a carefully drawn wordmark "xDev". This is a new design exploration, not trademark clearance.
Composition: a pristine opaque white landscape sheet with three equal vertical columns, enormous negative space, restrained editorial precision. Each column contains a small identifier "01", "02", or "03"; a large standalone monogram; beneath it a medium horizontal lockup with that exact monogram plus wordmark "xDev"; at the bottom, a small one-color black version and a tiny square app-icon application of that SAME monogram. Consistent scale and alignment across columns. Thin light-gray vertical separators only.
Concept 01: one confident continuous folded band, an angular lowercase x flowing seamlessly into a softly squared lowercase d. The d has a generous open counter. Architectural and carefully balanced, flat silhouette, no 3D.
Concept 02: a custom typographic ligature xd, an asymmetric lowercase d bowl and upright stem sharing a deliberately cut diagonal with x. Clever negative space, slightly rounded terminals, quiet editorial sophistication, very clear small-size readability.
Concept 03: two interlocking broad strokes that read x+d together, deliberate offset junction, a single distinctive notch. More dynamic and inventive but still simple and recognizable at 24px. No surrounding container except the app-icon example.
Wordmark exact spelling: "xDev" (lowercase x, uppercase D, lowercase e v). Medium-semibold custom contemporary sans, generous optical spacing, avoid exaggerated heavy bold. Do not use any existing company logo as reference.
Palette: large symbols electric blue #0759ED; wordmarks deep navy #17243D; lower monochrome symbols pure black. App icons blue field with white symbol. Flat colors. Opaque white #FFFFFF background.
Text only: "01", "02", "03", "xDev". No invented slogans, no product names on this exploration, no legal claims.
Constraints: crisp vector-like edges, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no texture, no perspective, no 3D, no four-petal X, no sparkle, no generic hexagon, no code brackets, no infinity loop, no recognizable social-network X. Actual professional logo design, not a font specimen. Prioritize original xd letter construction over decorative complexity.
