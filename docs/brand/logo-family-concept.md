# xDev logo family — concept 1

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. This PNG is an identity exploration, not a production vector asset. Existing website logos are unchanged.

## Family rules

- Preserve the four-module X silhouette and xDev wordmark across products.
- Use electric blue for the master identity.
- AI Studio changes only the upper-right module to teal and adds the product name.
- Future products may select one accent and a product name; do not distort the master geometry.
- Product names must carry meaning independently of color.
- Maintain monochrome and reversed marks, and use the symbol alone at icon sizes.
- Before production, redraw as SVG and check optical spacing, small-size readability, and contrast.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a polished identity concept presentation board for a NEW extensible software brand family, xDev. This is a design exploration, not a screenshot of a website.
Primary request: Design one original memorable master symbol for xDev and demonstrate a coherent AI Studio product derivative. The master identity must be recognizable without lettering, look excellent as a flat single-color favicon, and allow future software products to use the same silhouette with one controlled accent change.
Design direction: a confident, precisely constructed compact abstract X assembled from four broad interlocking folded chevron modules around a small diamond of negative space. Give the geometry a distinctive cut or offset, substantial optical weight, restrained rounded internal corners. Express building, connection, and modular software. Avoid a plain typeset X; avoid the X social-network logo; avoid AI sparkle cliches, brains, robots, circuits and generic hexagons. Make the same master silhouette EXACTLY consistent everywhere on the board. No tiny ornamental details.
Wordmark: exact text "xDev", custom-looking clean contemporary sans serif, lowercase x followed by uppercase D and lowercase ev, refined optical spacing. Product lockup: exact text "xDev" with "AI Studio" as a secondary name. Keep the product name visually subordinate.
Palette: master electric blue #0759ED and deep ink #17243D on cool white #F7F9FC. AI Studio inherits the same mark and blue, with just the upper-right module accented teal #18A999; also show a completely monochrome version. The color variation is the product customization, not a new unrelated icon.
Composition: high-resolution landscape brand designer presentation, spacious, exact grid, crisp flat vector-like edges. Top half is a large master symbol with xDev wordmark, generous breathing room, with a modest label "Master identity". Bottom half has three clean application zones: "AI Studio" with product lockup; "Monochrome" showing identical black mark on white and white mark on deep ink; "App icons" with master and product icons in modest rounded-square app tiles plus tiny favicon-size instances to demonstrate legibility. Minimal thin dividers. Editorial quality, precise alignment, strong contrast, no decorative background texture.
Text only: "xDev", "AI Studio", "Master identity", "Monochrome", "App icons". No other words, no watermark, no invented company claims. Flat logo design is primary, no 3D extrusion, no perspective mockup, no gradient on the core logo.
