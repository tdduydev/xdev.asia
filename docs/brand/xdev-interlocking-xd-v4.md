Three concepts on ONE landscape white design sheet, equally spaced columns:
01 — Enclosed: a clear upright capital D outline with a capital X elegantly nested inside its counter, the X's left endpoints thoughtfully integrated into the D's upright stem. Preserve generous space around the internal crossing. One coherent emblem, not an X pasted over a D.

03 — Negative space: the silhouette is an open rounded capital D whose interior counter reveals an X through precisely cut negative space. Light-medium optical weight, subtle asymmetry, not a heavy block.
Each column: a tiny gray number 01, 02, 03 above; large standalone monogram in deep navy #243047 at center; a SMALL neat wordmark "xDev" below in medium-weight refined sans-serif; at bottom a small 24px-like symbol application and a medium rounded-square blue app icon with the exact corresponding symbol in white. All instances within a column must use identical geometry. Real differences between the three concepts, not nearly identical duplicates.
Exact text only: "01", "02", "03", "xDev".
CRITICAL opaque white #FFFFFF background across the entire canvas, like white paper. No transparency. No gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no texture, no stock mockups. Navy logos, blue #2459A6 app tiles, gray labels. Generous margins and whitespace, thin very light gray vertical dividers.
